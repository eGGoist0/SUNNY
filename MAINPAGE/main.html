OUR PROJECT

Water pollution is a major issue caused by chemicals, plastics, and noise.
Many countries ignore the problem. Our project from our team 7 UP,
aims to remove excess CO2 from water, improving marine life conditions.
High CO2 harms corals and microorganisms, disrupting ecosystems.
7 UP will monitor and display water quality.
